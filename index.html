Privacy & data use

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy describes how Fresh House Market handles information for its storefront, business ordering, distribution, delivery, support and supplier communications.

This informational page does not provide shopping, account access or payments. It uses no analytics, advertising scripts or application sign-in cookies. Storefront cookie choices apply when you use the storefront.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you browse, create an account, place an order, contact support, save preferences, or use delivery and pickup features. This may include your name, email address, phone number, login details, delivery or pickup address, service-location preferences, order history, cart activity, substitution choices, support messages, and account settings.

2. Order, Delivery, and Location Information

We use order details, delivery instructions, address information, and service-area data to prepare products, verify coverage, coordinate delivery or pickup, send order updates, route drivers, handle substitutions, and resolve fulfillment issues. If a customer-facing tracking feature is available, it may show relevant order, route, or delivery status information for that order.

3. Payment Information

Payments are processed through payment service providers. Fresh House Market does not ask customers to send full card numbers or CVV codes through support messages. Where supported, we may store payment references or masked payment details, such as card brand, last four digits, expiration information, billing reference, and transaction status, so that orders, refunds, and receipts can be managed.

4. Device, Usage, and Cookie Data

We use necessary cookies and browser storage for account sessions, security, carts, orders, and your cookie choice. These functions remain available if you reject optional analytics. Google Analytics is optional and does not load until you choose Accept analytics. You can reject it or change your choice at any time using Cookie settings at the bottom of the site. If you withdraw consent, the page reloads to stop analytics and remove its first-party cookies. Browser settings can also control storage, though blocking necessary storage may affect sign-in and ordering.

5. How We Use Information

We use customer information to operate the storefront, create and manage accounts, process orders and payments, provide delivery and pickup, send service communications, respond to support requests, maintain product availability, personalize relevant shopping features, detect fraud or abuse, improve operations, and comply with legal, tax, accounting, safety, or regulatory obligations.

6. When We Share Information

We share information only as needed to run the service, complete orders, protect customers, or meet lawful obligations. This may include payment processors, authentication providers, hosting and database providers, analytics and messaging tools, map and delivery tools, drivers or fulfillment partners, professional advisers, and public authorities where required by law. We do not sell customer order history as part of normal storefront operations.

7. Marketing and Service Messages

We may send service messages about your account, orders, delivery, pickup, receipts, security, support, or policy changes. If promotional messaging is offered, you may opt out of marketing communications through the available unsubscribe or account preference tools. Service and transactional messages may still be sent when needed to operate your account or fulfill an order.

8. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including order fulfillment, account support, tax and accounting records, fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal compliance, and business records. When information is no longer needed, we may delete, de-identify, or archive it according to operational and legal requirements.

9. Your Choices and Requests

You can update many account details from your customer account. You may contact Fresh House Market to request help with account information, privacy questions, access, correction, deletion, account closure, or communication preferences. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where required for lawful, security, accounting, fraud-prevention, or order-resolution purposes.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect customer information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so keep your password private, use trusted devices where possible, and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

11. Children

The storefront is intended for customers who can lawfully purchase produce and manage an online account. We do not knowingly create customer accounts for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the storefront, contact us so we can review the request.

12. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services change. The current version will be posted with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@freshhousemarket.com or +1 (876) 352-1634. Our business dispatch address is 19 White Haven Drive, Kingston, Jamaica.

Facebook sign-in and WhatsApp supplier messages

When you choose Facebook sign-in, information returned by Facebook, such as your account identifier, name and email address, is handled through Supabase Auth to support your Fresh House account and sign-in. When you contact our WhatsApp Business account, we receive the sender number and available profile information, message contents and any attachments you provide. We use supplier messages to identify the supplier, prepare product availability, quantity, unit, price and readiness updates, and manage confirmation and staff review. We also use relevant communications to provide support and protect the service.

Service providers and recovery copies

Our service providers include Amazon Web Services for hosting and infrastructure, Supabase for database, authentication and files, Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) for service email, and Stripe for payment services. These providers may receive the account or service information needed for their functions, including Facebook-derived account contact details when used for the service. Recovery archives may be stored in iCloud Drive; these archives are encrypted before upload and their recovery keys are kept separately from the archives. GitHub Pages hosts this privacy document only; it does not host our application or account system. Our configured application and database hosting region is the United States; provider support and other operations may involve international processing under their applicable terms.

Requesting deletion of Facebook or WhatsApp-related data

To request access, correction or deletion, email support@freshhousemarket.com with the subject “Privacy data request”. Tell us whether your request concerns a customer account or supplier WhatsApp messages, and provide the account email or sender number needed to locate the relevant records. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or access tokens. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Records may be retained for the lawful purposes described above. You can also revoke Facebook permissions in your Facebook account’s Apps and websites settings; revoking permissions does not itself delete records already held by Fresh House Market.
